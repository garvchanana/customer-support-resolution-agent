# Damaged Item Support Policy

## Damage Report

When a customer reports that an item arrived damaged, collect
the order ID and a clear description of the damage.

If useful, ask the customer whether they can provide photographs
or other supporting evidence. Do not make this a mandatory
requirement unless an approved policy explicitly requires it.

## Refund and Replacement Decisions

A damage report alone does not establish refund eligibility.
Check the applicable return and refund policy before assessing
a refund.

Do not promise a replacement, refund, or compensation unless
the relevant business rules authorize that outcome.

## Human Review

Escalate cases that require a decision outside the available
business rules. Do not claim that a refund or replacement has
been approved when it has not.
