
# Refund and Returns Policy

Policy ID: REFUND-DEMO-001
Version: demo-v1

## Return window
A return must be within 30 calendar days of the order's
delivery date. Requests outside this window are not eligible
under this demo policy.

## Return receipt
The returned product must be received before a refund can
be approved. A requested or in-transit return does not count
as received.

## Product inspection
The returned product must pass inspection before a refund
can be approved. A pending or failed inspection does not
satisfy this requirement.

## Payment and refund amount
The order must have a successful payment. The refundable
amount cannot exceed the amount originally paid minus
previous refunds.

## Human approval
Every refund requires human approval. An eligibility
assessment is not approval, and the system must not issue
a refund merely because an order is eligible.

## Missing evidence
If required order, return, payment, or inspection information
is missing, do not assume the requirement has been satisfied.
Request further investigation or human review.
